# Crafty Business Facts

Internal project/repository name: `crafty`

Customer-facing business name: **Breezy's Creative Co.**

Public website copy, branding, metadata, and customer-facing references should
use **Breezy's Creative Co.** unless a technical context specifically refers to
the repository/project.

## Business model

Crafty is a home-based, online-first artist and maker business.

Bre is the primary artist/maker. The business and its technical infrastructure
are being developed and operated from home.

The website has three core purposes:

1. Artist portfolio
2. Commission/custom-work lead generation
3. Storefront for ready-to-buy work

The people running the business are full-time caregivers, so available time is
limited. Flea markets, festivals, and physical events may happen eventually but
must not be treated as the primary business model.

## Current making capabilities

- Heat-transfer-vinyl T-shirts
- Paintings
- Epoxy resin work using molds
- D&D dice
- Beach-sand and shell resin coasters
- Bead bracelets

## Available equipment

- Cricut Explore 3
- Heat press
- Vinyl
- Easel
- Paints
- Projector for painting outlines/reference work
- General arts-and-crafts supplies

## Known sales history

- Custom shirts have been sold for money.
- Some prior orders came through church/community connections.
- A friend in Ohio previously ordered shirts for a soccer team.
- A previous Etsy attempt produced zero sales and had essentially no marketing
  or advertising effort.

## Current operating direction

- Keep overhead low.
- Build from home.
- Use the website as the permanent business hub.
- Portfolio work does not need to be currently for sale.
- Some work can be sold as ready-made inventory.
- Custom shirts and paintings can be handled through commission/custom-work
  inquiries.
- Marketing should initially emphasize actual finished work, word of mouth,
  community connections, and online outreach.
- Paid advertising may be tested later, after the website, portfolio, offers,
  and basic organic presence are ready. It is not part of the initial
  implementation.
## Current shirt-pricing thinking

- Shirt blanks currently cost roughly $3–$5.
- Vinyl should eventually be costed internally by material area used.
- Number of colors alone should not determine vinyl price; total material use
  and actual additional labor matter.
- A final public pricing model has not been established.
